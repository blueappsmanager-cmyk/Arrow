1 Overview & Commitment

Arrow Snake Puzzle (hereinafter referred to as "the Game", "the Application", or "We") is developed by BlueAppBox. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to safeguarding user privacy and data security in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA guidelines.

2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you run Arrow Snake Puzzle on your device, standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data may be collected automatically by verified third-party SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob):

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version (e.g. Android 13/14), screen resolution, system language, and network connection type.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) utilized by Google AdMob for delivering and frequency-capping advertisements.
  • Performance & Crash Logs: Diagnostic information regarding game stability, frame rates, and crash logs to troubleshoot and improve game performance.

💡 Notice: This automated information cannot identify you personally in the real world and is only utilized to provide a seamless gaming experience and reward advertisements.

3 What We NEVER Collect

Arrow Snake Puzzle is a casual puzzle game that functions without requiring personal accounts or intrusive permissions. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your real name, postal address, physical location, or national ID.
  • Your contact book, phone numbers, email accounts, or SMS messages.
  • Financial details, bank account credentials, credit/debit card numbers.
  • Access to your camera, microphone, gallery photos, or audio recordings.

5 Offline Game Data & Local Storage

All gameplay progress—including your current unlocked level (1 to 300), collected stars, unlocked snake skins, remaining hints, and audio settings—is stored locally on your device using browser/DOM LocalStorage.

  • Your game save data remains completely on your phone and is not uploaded to our remote servers.
  • You can reset or wipe this game progress at any time by going to your Android device settings: Settings → Apps → Arrow Snake Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data.
